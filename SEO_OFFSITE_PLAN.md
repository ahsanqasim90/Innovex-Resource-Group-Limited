# Innovex Resource Group Off-Site SEO & Authority Plan

Off-site authority cannot be created credibly through code. This plan focuses on genuine relationships, useful expertise, accurate business listings and measurable referral value. Avoid paid link packages, private blog networks, automated directory submissions, irrelevant guest posts, comment spam and fabricated reviews.

## Foundation

1. Verify one canonical public business name, phone, address, website and opening hours with the business owner.
2. Align the website, Google Business Profile, Bing Places and genuine company/industry listings.
3. Claim and secure Search Console/Bing Webmaster Tools properties for both domain and canonical `www` host where appropriate.
4. Add UTM conventions for partnerships, profiles and campaigns without placing personal data in URLs.
5. Record every target, contact, status, earned URL, anchor/context, referral sessions and qualified leads in a simple outreach register.

## Recruitment authority opportunities

- Contribute genuinely useful employer guidance to UK care, nursing, supported-living and children’s residential publications.
- Seek profiles in reputable recruitment and care-sector organisations/directories where Innovex meets the membership criteria.
- Offer expert commentary on recruitment timescales, candidate experience, hard-to-fill roles and workforce planning—only using verified experience/data.
- Create a high-quality employer resource, such as a vacancy-brief template or interview scorecard, reviewed by a recruitment professional.
- Ask genuine clients/partners with permission to reference the specific recruitment relationship on their websites or supplier pages.
- Develop local relationships with Cardiff/Wales business organisations while maintaining UK-wide employer positioning.

## Digital and SEO authority opportunities

- Publish technical breakdowns of approved Innovex projects, focusing on problem, architecture, accessibility and delivery decisions rather than invented results.
- Contribute to React, Node/MERN, web performance and small-business digital publications where the team has genuine expertise.
- Release a useful open-source component, checklist or non-sensitive benchmark with clear documentation.
- Seek partner/supplier attribution from genuine client websites only when it is contractually and editorially appropriate.
- Submit approved projects to relevant design/development showcases only where the project and client permit it.
- Use original research only when the methodology, sample and limitations can be published transparently.

## Training authority opportunities

- Obtain accurate listings from genuine awarding bodies/accreditors only after provider/course status is verified.
- Build relationships with care-provider networks and professional-development communities.
- Create reviewed training-planning resources for managers rather than making unsupported “mandatory” claims.
- Ask organisational training clients for genuine feedback and permission to publish it, with no incentive tied to a positive review.

## Digital PR ideas

- Quarterly, anonymised hiring-demand observations based on sufficient internal data, with methodology and privacy review.
- An annual care-employer recruitment challenges survey with transparent sample size and questions.
- A technical teardown of common care-provider website accessibility/conversion issues using public data and respectful methodology.
- A training-needs survey for care managers, avoiding regulatory conclusions unless independently reviewed.

Do not publish internal data if the sample is too small, identifiable or operationally sensitive.

## Review strategy

- Ask for reviews only from people/organisations with a genuine experience.
- Use the platform most relevant to the relationship; do not copy a review between platforms without permission.
- Do not gate review requests based on expected sentiment.
- Respond professionally without disclosing candidate, client, placement or training details.
- Do not add self-serving review schema unless current search-engine eligibility and structured-data rules support it.

## 90-day off-site cadence

### Weeks 1–2

- Confirm NAP/brand details and profile ownership.
- Audit existing citations, referring domains and harmful/spam links without paying for removal packages.
- Build a list of 20 high-relevance organisations/publications split across recruitment, digital and training.

### Weeks 3–6

- Publish one genuinely useful, reviewed resource connected to a money page.
- Contact a small number of highly relevant organisations with a tailored contribution proposal.
- Secure permissions/data for the first digital or recruitment case study.

### Weeks 7–12

- Publish the approved case study/resource.
- Continue relationship-led outreach; target quality and referral relevance, not link volume.
- Review earned referral traffic, enquiries and assisted conversions.
- Drop channels producing irrelevant traffic or low-quality enquiries.

## Measurement

Track:

- relevant new referring domains and pages;
- branded search growth;
- referral traffic and qualified enquiries;
- assisted conversions by business division;
- rankings/impressions for the exact linked commercial topic;
- profile views, calls and website visits from legitimate local/business profiles;
- cost and staff time per qualified opportunity.

Do not use raw backlink count or domain-rating changes as the sole success metric.
