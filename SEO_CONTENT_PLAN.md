# Innovex Resource Group SEO Content Plan

This plan prioritises commercial usefulness and qualified leads. It is a publishing roadmap, not permission to mass-generate articles. Before publication, validate topics with Search Console, keyword tools, sales questions and the actual services/courses Innovex can evidence. Improve any existing article targeting the same intent instead of creating a competing URL.

Priority key: P1 = supports a current money page; P2 = authority/growth after money pages; P3 = needs business/legal input.

## Existing blog audit workflow

For every published database record, export or record: URL, title, H1, meta title, meta description, published/updated dates, category, word count, primary intent, target service, internal links and CTA. Then classify it as keep, improve, consolidate, redirect or remove.

Check Search Console before changing a URL. Prioritise pages with impressions and positions 5–20, low CTR, backlinks or existing leads. Do not consolidate pages solely because titles look similar; compare query intent and content first.

## Editorial quality gate

Every article must:

- be reviewed by a person with relevant Innovex experience;
- avoid unsupported legal, compliance, pricing and outcome claims;
- answer the search intent before promoting a service;
- link to one primary commercial page and a small number of genuinely relevant supporting pages;
- use a specific CTA aligned with recruitment, digital, SEO or training;
- include an author/reviewer and meaningful reviewed/updated date;
- be checked against existing articles for cannibalisation;
- have unique metadata and a single descriptive H1.
